Important Safety Information: Who Cannot Take Cialis?

Absolute Contraindications — Do NOT Take Cialis If:
  • You take any nitrate medication (nitroglycerin spray/patch/tablet, isosorbide mononitrate, isosorbide dinitrate) — this combination causes severe, potentially fatal hypotension
  • You take riociguat (Adempas) for pulmonary hypertension
  • You have had a heart attack or stroke in the past 90 days
  • You have unstable angina or chest pain during sexual activity
  • You have severe liver impairment (Child-Pugh Class C)

Use With Caution If You Take:

  • Alpha-blockers (tamsulosin, doxazosin) — can lower blood pressure; start at 5mg tadalafil
  • Antihypertensives (blood pressure medications) — additive lowering effect
  • CYP3A4 inhibitors (ketoconazole, ritonavir, erythromycin) — raise tadalafil blood levels; dose reduction may be needed

Side Effects of Tadalafil (Cialis)

Tadalafil's side effect profile differs slightly from sildenafil because it is less selective for PDE5 versus PDE11 (found in muscle and testicular tissue):

Common Side Effects

  • Back pain and muscle aches — tadalafil's signature side effect, caused by PDE11 inhibition in muscle tissue. Typically appears 12–24 hours after taking and resolves within 48 hours.
  • Headache — from vasodilation
  • Flushing — warmth and redness in the face
  • Nasal congestion
  • Indigestion or heartburn
  • Dizziness

Less Common

  • Visual disturbances — less common than with sildenafil (tadalafil has better PDE6 selectivity)
  • Priapism (prolonged erection >4 hours) — rare but requires immediate emergency treatment
  • Sudden vision or hearing loss — very rare; seek emergency help immediately

Back pain and muscle aches are the most distinctively reported side effect of tadalafil vs sildenafil or vardenafil. If this is bothersome, vardenafil (Levitra) or sildenafil may be better tolerated. See our ED medication side effects guide for a full comparison.

How to Take Cialis for Best Results

Once you have your medication:

  • On-demand (10mg or 20mg): Take 30–60 minutes before sexual activity. Unlike Viagra, a heavy meal does not significantly affect absorption. Avoid grapefruit juice (inhibits metabolism).
  • Daily (2.5mg or 5mg): Take at the same time each day, regardless of planned sexual activity. After 5–7 days of daily use, steady-state levels are reached and spontaneous erections become possible at any time.

  • Patience: Give on-demand Cialis at least 6–8 attempts before concluding it is ineffective for you. Daily dosing typically needs 3–4 weeks of consistent use to assess full benefit.
