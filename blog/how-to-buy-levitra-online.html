Levitra (Vardenafil) — At a Glance

  • Generic name: Vardenafil hydrochloride
  • Drug class: PDE5 inhibitor

  • Onset: 25–60 minutes after taking
  • Duration of action: 4–8 hours
  • Prescription required: Yes — in the US, Levitra requires a valid prescription
  • Available as generic: Yes — generic vardenafil is widely available and equally effective
Key Takeaway: Levitra is a prescription-only medication in the United States. Any legitimate online pharmacy will require either a current prescription or will connect you with a licensed prescriber before dispensing. Step 2: Complete a Medical Consultation (If Required)

If you do not have an existing prescription for vardenafil, you will need to complete a medical consultation. Reputable online pharmacies offer this digitally — you fill out a health questionnaire that is reviewed by a licensed physician. The questionnaire typically covers:

Vardenafil is absolutely contraindicated with nitrate medications (nitroglycerin spray, isosorbide), and combining the two can cause a sudden, life-threatening drop in blood pressure. Red Flags: How to Spot an Unsafe Online Pharmacy

Unfortunately, the internet is full of illegitimate websites selling counterfeit or substandard ED medications. These rogue pharmacies pose serious health risks. Avoid any website that:

Warning — Do Not Buy From Sites That:
  • Sell prescription medications without requiring a prescription or consultation
  • Offer Levitra at prices that seem too good to be true (e.g., $0.50 per pill)
  • Have no verifiable US address or phone number
  • Are not listed in the NABP verified database or LegitScript certified
  • Accept only wire transfers, cryptocurrency, or money orders as payment
  • Have a website with poor English, spelling errors, or no medical disclaimers
  • Send unsolicited emails advertising ED medications

Counterfeit Levitra is a genuine problem. Studies by the FDA and WHO have found that a significant proportion of ED medications purchased from unverified online sources contain incorrect amounts of the active ingredient, wrong substances entirely, or no active ingredient at all. In the worst cases, counterfeit tablets have been found to contain dangerous undisclosed substances.

How to Take Levitra for Best Results

Once you have your medication, taking it correctly will maximise its effectiveness:

  • Take your Levitra tablet 25–60 minutes before sexual activity
  • Avoid high-fat meals before taking — fatty food significantly delays absorption and reduces the peak effect
  • Alcohol in moderation is acceptable; heavy drinking impairs both the medication and natural sexual function
  • Remember that sexual stimulation is required — vardenafil does not cause automatic erections
  • Take no more than one dose every 24 hours
  • Give it at least 6–8 attempts before deciding whether it is working for you; first-time results are not always representative
